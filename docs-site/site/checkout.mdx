---
title: "Checkout"
description: "The hosted page you send a subscriber to, and how they come back."
---

Elapse hosts one page in your flow: the checkout. You create a session on your server, send the subscriber to its URL, and they return to you when the meter stops. You never hold a card, a wallet, or a balance.

## Creating a session

<CodeGroup>
```ts TypeScript
const session = await elapse.checkout.sessions.create({
  product: "prod_…",
  successUrl: "https://yourapp.example/ok",
  cancelUrl: "https://yourapp.example/cancel",
  maxDurationSeconds: 3600, // optional cap; default one hour
});
redirect(session.url);
```
```bash cURL
curl "$ELAPSE_API_URL/v1/checkout/sessions" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ELAPSE_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"product":"prod_…","success_url":"https://yourapp.example/ok","cancel_url":"https://yourapp.example/cancel","max_duration_seconds":3600}'
```
</CodeGroup>

`max_duration_seconds` caps how long one subscription can run and therefore how much the subscriber is asked to fund: `rate × cap`, shown to them as a dollar amount before they start. When the cap is reached the meter stops on its own.

## What the subscriber sees

The page is built for a phone and says nothing about chains. In order:

1. **Your product and its rate.** Your name and logo from the dashboard, the product, `$0.004 / second` and what that is per hour.
2. **Sign in.** Face ID or a passkey. No password, no seed phrase, no app to install.
3. **Funds.** The amount that covers the cap, in dollars. Unused funds return the moment they cancel.
4. **Start.** A live meter: seconds elapsed and dollars accrued, ticking, no jitter.
5. **Cancel.** A receipt: "You paid 83 seconds · $0.33". Then the redirect to your `success_url`.

If they leave before pressing Start, or the session's hour passes, they land on `cancel_url` and nothing was charged.

## Coming back

Elapse appends the session id to your success URL:

```text
https://yourapp.example/ok?session_id=cs_7Ha…
```

Do not grant access from that redirect alone; a URL can be typed. Grant access when `subscription.created` arrives at your webhook endpoint, and revoke it on `subscription.canceled`. The [Quickstart](/quickstart) handler does exactly that with an in-memory map keyed by subscription id.

## Session states

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `open` | Created, not yet started. Expires after one hour. |
| `complete` | The subscriber pressed Start. A Subscription exists and `checkout.session.completed` has fired. |
| `expired` | Nobody started it within the hour. Create a new one. |

A session is single-use. Create one per visit, not one per product.

## The promise

Whatever happens on the chain underneath, the subscriber only ever sees seconds and dollars. There is one exception, a judge mode on the receipt that shows the settlement transaction for people who want to verify it. Your integration never has to mention it.
